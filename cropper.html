Free Online Image Cropper

UtilityCanvas Image Cropper lets you crop JPG, PNG and WebP images directly in your browser without uploading your files to a server.

How to crop an image

  • Choose a JPG, PNG or WebP image.
  • Set the crop X and Y position.
  • Set the crop width and height.
  • Use 1:1, 16:9 or 4:3 for a quick crop ratio.
  • Select your preferred output format.
  • Click Crop Image.
  • Download the cropped image.

Private browser-based processing

Your image is processed directly inside your browser. Your image does not need to be uploaded to a server.

Supported formats

JPG, PNG and WebP images are supported for cropping.

Common uses for image cropping

  • Crop photos to remove unwanted areas or backgrounds.
  • Prepare images for social media posts and profile pictures.
  • Crop pictures to square, 16:9 or 4:3 ratios.
  • Remove extra space around a subject before resizing an image.
  • Prepare images for websites, documents and online forms.

Frequently asked questions

Can I crop an image online for free?

Yes. UtilityCanvas lets you crop JPG, PNG and WebP images directly in your browser for free.

Can I crop an image to a specific ratio?

Yes. You can use the Free option or choose 1:1, 16:9 or 4:3 quick crop ratios.

Can I crop images on my phone?

Yes. The Image Cropper works in modern mobile browsers as well as on tablets and desktop devices.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. Image processing happens directly in your browser, so your image does not need to be uploaded to a server.
