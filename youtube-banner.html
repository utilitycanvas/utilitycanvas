Create YouTube-ready channel banners

Resize images to the recommended YouTube channel banner dimensions: 2560 × 1440 or the minimum 2048 × 1152.

YouTube recommends 2560 × 1440 pixels for the best display across devices. Important text and logos should stay inside the safe area because the banner can be cropped differently on different devices.
