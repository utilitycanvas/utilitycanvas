Resize Images Online for Free

UtilityCanvas Image Resizer lets you resize images directly in your browser without uploading your files to a server.

How to resize an image

  • Choose a JPG, PNG or WebP image.
  • Enter the desired width and height.
  • Keep the lock enabled to preserve the original aspect ratio.
  • Select your preferred output format and quality.
  • Click Resize Image.
  • Download your resized image.

Private browser-based processing

Your image is processed directly inside your browser. The image does not need to be uploaded to a server for resizing.

Supported formats

JPG, PNG and WebP images are supported for resizing and conversion.
