Free YouTube Profile Picture Resizer

UtilityCanvas YouTube Profile Picture Resizer lets you create square profile pictures directly in your browser without uploading your image to a server.

How to resize a YouTube profile picture

  • Choose a JPG, PNG or WebP image.
  • Select 800 × 800, 400 × 400 or 200 × 200.
  • Adjust the width and height if needed.
  • Select your preferred output format.
  • Choose image quality.
  • Click Resize Profile Picture.
  • Download the finished image.

Automatic center cropping

The image is cropped from the center into a square without stretching or distorting the original image.

Private browser-based processing

Your image is processed directly inside your browser. Your image does not need to be uploaded to a server.
