Free YouTube Thumbnail Resizer

UtilityCanvas YouTube Thumbnail Resizer lets you resize images to common YouTube thumbnail dimensions directly in your browser without uploading your files to a server.

How to resize a YouTube thumbnail

  • Choose a JPG, PNG or WebP image.
  • Select 1280 × 720, 1920 × 1080 or 3840 × 2160.
  • Adjust the width and height if needed.
  • Select your preferred output format.
  • Choose image quality.
  • Click Resize Thumbnail.
  • Download the finished thumbnail.

Private browser-based processing

Your image is processed directly inside your browser. Your image does not need to be uploaded to a server.

Recommended YouTube thumbnail size

1280 × 720 pixels is a common 16:9 YouTube thumbnail size.
